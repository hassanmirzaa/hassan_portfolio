# Portfolio Redesign Prompt (copy everything below the line)

---

You are a senior design engineer who has worked at studios like Locomotive, Active Theory and Linear, and who also ships production mobile apps. You are redesigning the personal portfolio of **Hassan Mirza**, a Full Stack Mobile Engineer. The result must look like it was **art-directed by a human with taste**, not generated by an AI. A visitor should feel a specific person made this.

## 1. Who the site is for

- **Primary audience:** founders, CTOs and hiring managers who need a mobile app built end to end (Flutter frontend plus Laravel / Node.js backend). They skim for 20 seconds, then decide whether to email.
- **Single goal:** get them to email me or book a call. Every section should lead to that.
- **Positioning (one sentence, use it as the hero idea):** "I build mobile apps and the backends behind them, alone, from first screen to App Store." Don't use "passionate", "pixel perfect", "cutting-edge" or "innovative".

## 2. Real content (use exactly this, don't invent anything)

- **Name:** Hassan Mirza. **Role:** Full Stack Mobile Engineer, 3+ years. **Current:** Ismail Industries Ltd, 2023 to present (Flutter apps with Laravel and Node.js APIs, Firebase / Supabase).
- **Stack:** Flutter, Dart, Riverpod / Bloc / Provider, Laravel (PHP), Node.js / Express, MySQL, Firebase, Supabase, OpenAI API, Google Maps, Pusher, App Store / Play Store deployment.
- **Projects (4):**
  1. **Waterverse Connect:** customer app for water delivery. Order tracking on Google Maps, payments, push notifications, deals, support. Flutter + Laravel API + Firebase.
  2. **AI Workout Planner:** generates workout plans from goals and equipment, tracks progress, adapts to feedback. Flutter + Node.js + Supabase + OpenAI.
  3. **Orange POS & Delivery System:** POS, inventory, real-time GPS delivery tracking, geo-fenced zones, dynamic discounts, complaints. Flutter + Laravel + MySQL + Pusher + SQLite offline.
  4. **Tusai (AI recipe generator):** recipes from the ingredients you have, shopping lists, dietary preferences, ad monetisation. Flutter + Firebase + OpenAI + Google Ads.
- Existing assets: `/public/hassan.png`, `/public/waterverse-connect.png`, `/public/ai-workout-planner.png`, `/public/tusai.png`.
- **Placeholders:** wherever I haven't given a fact (metrics, store links, quotes, email), put a clearly marked `TODO:` in the code. **Never fabricate testimonials, client names, download counts or stats.** Cut any section I have no real content for.

## 3. What is wrong with the current site (do the opposite)

The current site is a Next.js / shadcn template with the usual AI-generated fingerprints. Avoid every one of these:

- Navy background with cyan and indigo neon accents, and gradient text on headings (`from-primary to-secondary bg-clip-text`).
- Glassmorphism cards with glow borders on every block; a cursor glow, a floating particle background, a top scroll progress bar, a floating CTA bubble and a chatbot widget all at once.
- Emoji used as icons (📱🐘🟢🔥⚡🚀).
- A centered "Title + subtitle + grid of identical cards" repeated for every section.
- Fake-feeling sections: stats row, testimonials, process steps, FAQ, blog list. Poppins/Inter-style default typography, ✓ checklists, "Let's launch pixel perfect apps in just days" with a strikethrough gimmick.
- Everything fading up on scroll with the same stagger.
- Every section being a rounded-lg card.

## 4. Design direction

Pick **one** bold, specific direction and commit to it. Don't blend them. My recommendation: **"Editorial engineer's notebook"**: warm and typographic, closer to a well-made printed magazine or a developer's field notes than to a SaaS landing page.

- **Palette:** warm off-white paper (`#F2EEE6`-ish) with near-black ink (`#14130F`), plus **one** sharp accent used sparingly (a hot vermilion or signal orange, for example). Optional dark mode that is its own considered palette, not an inversion. No gradients, no neon, no purple.
- **Typography is the design.** One characterful display serif or grotesk (e.g. Fraunces, Instrument Serif, PP Editorial-style, or Space Grotesk / Neue Montreal-style) at very large sizes with tight tracking, paired with a refined sans for body and a mono (JetBrains Mono / Geist Mono) for metadata, labels, indices and dates. Use real typographic detail: optical sizes, small caps for labels, hanging numerals, proper quotes and dashes.
- **Layout:** a strict 12-column grid with intentional asymmetry, oversized type bleeding across columns, thin 1px rules instead of card boxes, generous white space, and numbered sections (`01 / Work`). Vary each section's layout so no two look alike. Sections shouldn't all be centered.
- **Imagery:** show the apps properly. Put real screenshots in device frames or cropped edge to edge, and for each project make one hero shot plus 2-3 supporting screens. Avoid stock illustrations and abstract blobs.
- **Texture:** subtle paper grain at about 3-4% opacity, and tiny imperfections that suggest a human (a hand-set annotation, an underline drawn in SVG, a slightly rotated sticker-like label). Use these once or twice, not everywhere.

## 5. Structure (short, in this order)

1. **Header:** wordmark "Hassan Mirza" on the left; on the right `Work, About, Contact` and a live local time (Karachi) in mono. Sticky, minimal, no blur pill.
2. **Hero:** one huge sentence of type (the positioning line), a small line of availability ("Available for new projects from <month>"), and one text-link CTA to email. No buttons stacked in a row, no stats.
3. **Selected work (the centerpiece):** the four projects as a large editorial index. Each row shows number, name, one-line outcome, stack in mono and year. Hovering reveals the app screenshot following the cursor or expanding in place. Clicking opens a **case study page** (`/projects/[slug]`) with: the problem, my role (design, Flutter, backend, deploy), architecture notes, 2-3 decisions and tradeoffs, screens, and store links `TODO`. This depth is what separates real engineers from AI-looking portfolios.
4. **How I work / Stack:** not a skills grid with logos. Write it as a short paragraph and a typographic list grouped by *Mobile, Backend, Data, Shipping*, in mono.
5. **About:** a real photo (`hassan.png`, treated well: grayscale or duotone, cropped asymmetrically) with 2 short paragraphs in my own plain voice. Include one human, specific detail. Ask me for it if you don't have one.
6. **Experience:** a simple typographic timeline, one entry now.
7. **Contact / footer:** giant email address as the link, socials as plain text links (`TODO` URLs), local time, and `© 2026`. Keep the working contact form route (`/api/send-email`) but make the form minimal: underlined fields, no rounded boxes.

Remove: testimonials, FAQ, process, hero stats, blogs, floating CTA, chatbot, cursor glow, particle background, progress bar. (Keep `/api/*` routes in place; just don't render them.)

## 6. Motion

- Restraint. One signature moment (e.g. hero text revealing by line with a mask, or project rows with a smooth image reveal) plus clean micro-interactions.
- Use Lenis-style smooth scroll only if it's tuned well. Use easing like `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`, durations of 600-900ms for reveals and 150-250ms for hovers. Do not use bounce or spring overshoot on text.
- Page transitions between home and case studies should be a quick, smooth, shared-element or clip transition.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` everywhere.

## 7. Technical requirements

- Keep Next.js 16 (App Router), React 19, Tailwind v4, TypeScript. Remove unused shadcn `components/ui/*` files and unused dependencies (recharts, react-day-picker, etc.) to keep the bundle tiny.
- Load fonts with `next/font`; use `next/image` with correct `sizes`, AVIF/WebP, and no layout shift.
- Define design tokens (colors, type scale, spacing) as CSS variables in `globals.css`. No arbitrary magic numbers scattered through components.
- Semantic HTML, visible focus states, AA contrast, full keyboard navigation, alt text on every image.
- Mobile first. The design must be *redesigned* for 375px (not merely stacked): large type still reads, project rows become tap-friendly, and hover effects have touch equivalents.
- Per-page metadata, Open Graph image (generated with `next/og` in the same visual style), sitemap, and JSON-LD `Person` schema.
- Lighthouse targets: Performance 95+, Accessibility 100, SEO 100. LCP under 2s.
- Do not use `"use client"` on a component unless it needs state or effects.

## 8. Process and quality bar

1. Before writing code, state the design concept in 5 lines: the idea, palette with hex values, the type pairing, the grid, and the signature interaction. Wait for my OK.
2. Build the hero and one project row first and show me. Then continue.
3. After finishing, run the app and review it at 375px, 768px and 1440px. List anything that still looks "template-y" and fix it.
4. **Self-check against this list before you finish.** If any answer is yes, redo it:
   - Could this be mistaken for a Vercel / v0 / shadcn starter?
   - Are there gradients, glows, glass cards or emoji icons?
   - Do two sections share the same layout?
   - Is any text generic enough to appear on any developer's site?
   - Is there any invented stat, quote or client?
5. Commit in small, well-named steps.

Write copy in first person, plain and specific, short sentences, no buzzwords, with a light dry sense of humour at most once.
